Helping Katy families bathe safely

Katy grew from a small rice-farming town into one of the largest suburban areas west of Houston, and many people who moved here to raise children are now planning for their own later years, or for a parent who has moved in. That shows up in our calls: a mother-in-law suite that needs a safer tub, a primary bath upstairs that has become a climb, or a garden tub nobody can get out of anymore.

We start with a phone conversation, measure the bathroom, and give you a written quote that lists the tub, removal, plumbing, electrical and wall work. You decide with all the facts in front of you.

What we see in Katy-area houses

Two-story plans with the primary bath upstairs

A large share of homes built in the Katy area from the 1990s onward are two stories. When the primary suite is upstairs, we check the floor framing for the weight of a filled tub and a bather, and we measure the stair turn the tub has to pass. Sometimes the better answer is converting a downstairs bath, especially if the person bathing should not be climbing stairs at all.

Garden tubs in large primary baths

Deep, wide garden tubs were popular in many Katy-area builds. They look inviting but are hard to climb in and out of. These rooms usually have space for a roomy walk-in tub, a transfer tub with an outward door, or a generous roll-in shower.

Slab foundations

Like most of the region, Katy homes sit on slabs. Matching the tub's drain side to the existing drain keeps costs down.

Weather and water around Katy

West Houston and the Katy area sit near the Addicks and Barker reservoirs and along creeks and bayous that rise in heavy rain. During Harvey in 2017, some neighborhoods near the reservoirs flooded for days. If your home took on water before, tell us. We will look closely at the walls and subfloor behind the old tub and suggest materials that tolerate moisture. Our flood and storm guide covers power outages too.

Permits and rules: three counties, several jurisdictions

"Katy" on an envelope can mean the City of Katy, unincorporated Harris County, unincorporated Fort Bend County or Waller County. Inside Katy city limits, the city's building department handles permits. Outside city limits, county and utility district rules apply, and many master-planned communities also have deed restrictions or HOA rules, which usually matter for exterior work rather than a bathroom. When plumbing lines move or circuits are added, a licensed plumber and electrician should do that work and handle any permits the jurisdiction requires.

Local help for older adults in the Katy area

Which Area Agency on Aging serves you depends on the county your address is in:

  • Harris County addresses: Harris County Area Agency on Aging, run by the Houston Health Department, 832-393-4301.
  • Fort Bend or Waller County addresses: H-GAC Area Agency on Aging, 1-800-437-7396.

Both offer benefits counseling, caregiver support and referrals for people 60 and older. Veterans can work with the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center and its community clinics on HISA home modification grants.

Tub or shower for a Katy home?

If the person loves a long soak and can manage a small step, a walk-in tub is often a good fit. If a walker or wheelchair is part of daily life, or if a caregiver helps with every bath, a roll-in shower in a large primary bath may serve better for years. Our comparison guide walks through it.

Older adults around Katy, by the numbers

The Census Bureau counts the City of Katy and the Cinco Ranch area separately, and both are small compared with the wider area people call Katy. In the 2019 to 2023 American Community Survey, about 2,900 City of Katy residents (12.0%) and about 2,500 Cinco Ranch residents (13.2%) were 65 or older. Across Fort Bend County the figure was about 104,600 people. Fulshear, one of the fastest-growing nearby cities, has a much younger profile, with 8.4% of residents 65 or older. More county and city figures are on our Houston older adults data page.

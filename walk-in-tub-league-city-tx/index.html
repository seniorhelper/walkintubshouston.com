Bay Area families, safer bathrooms

League City and its neighbors grew up around the space center, the marinas and the refineries, and many of the engineers, teachers and boaters who settled here are now retirees. A lot of them want to stay put near the water. A walk-in tub or a low-threshold shower can be a big part of making that possible.

We talk through needs by phone, measure, and give a written quote covering the tub and all of the work around it.

Homes and bathrooms in League City

Older neighborhoods near Main Street

Some homes near the historic center are decades old, with small bathrooms and older plumbing. We measure doorways and check what is behind the tub when it comes out.

South Shore Harbour and newer subdivisions

Newer neighborhoods have larger primary baths and many two-story plans. These rooms often fit a roomy walk-in tub or a roll-in shower with a fold-down seat.

Hurricanes, surge and salt air

Galveston County has lived through Ike in 2008, Harvey in 2017 and Beryl in 2024, among others. Clear Creek and Dickinson Bayou can flood in heavy rain, and storm surge reaches low areas near the bay. If your home has flooded, tell us before we quote. We look at the subfloor and wall cavity and can suggest surround materials that handle moisture better.

Salt air and humidity are hard on metal and seals over time. Wiping the door gasket after each bath, running a vented exhaust fan and keeping an eye on caulk lines help a walk-in tub last. Our storm planning guide covers power outages, too: the tub's door, seat and gravity drain still work without electricity, but jets and heaters do not.

Permits in League City

Inside League City limits, the city's building department handles building, plumbing and electrical permits. Moving drain lines, adding circuits or rebuilding walls typically needs licensed trades and a permit. Webster, Seabrook, Kemah, Dickinson and Friendswood each have their own city offices, so the rules can change a few blocks away. We confirm what applies to your address.

Local help for older adults

  • Galveston County addresses: H-GAC Area Agency on Aging, 1-800-437-7396.
  • Harris County addresses such as Webster and Clear Lake: Harris County Area Agency on Aging, 832-393-4301.
  • Veterans: the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center and its community clinics; ask about HISA grants.
  • Disaster recovery: 2-1-1 Texas lists repair and recovery help after storms.

Tub, shower or both?

Many Bay Area couples keep a standard tub in a guest bath and convert the primary bath to a walk-in tub or roll-in shower. If one partner needs no-step access and the other loves to soak, that split can work well. We will help you weigh it.

After a storm

If water reaches the bathroom, do not use jets, heated seats or in-line heaters until the wiring has been checked. Photograph damage for insurance before cleanup, and call us to inspect the tub, door seal and surround. 2-1-1 Texas lists recovery help when it is available.

An older Bay Area

Galveston County has the highest share of older residents among the region's larger counties: 15.3% were 65 or older in the Census Bureau's 2019 to 2023 American Community Survey, compared with 12.0% across the metro. In League City itself the figure was 12.4%, about 14,200 people, while Webster (14.2%) and Seabrook (14.4%) ran a little higher. More figures are on our Houston older adults data page.

Houston freeze prep for your walk-in tub and pipes

A short checklist for the few nights a year that matter.

How often does Houston actually freeze?

Not often, which is exactly why freezes catch people off guard. The National Weather Service office for Houston and Galveston publishes long-term cold statistics for its main stations. Inland gets colder than the coast, so a family in Spring or Cypress sees more freezing nights than a family in League City or Galveston.

The number that matters most for plumbing is the hard freeze. The City of Houston averages 2 nights a year at or below 25°F and hits 25°F in 57% of years. Those are the nights when a pipe in an outside wall or an unheated attic is at real risk, and when a cold bathroom can chill the water left inside a walk-in tub's plumbing.

What in a walk-in tub can freeze?

The acrylic or gelcoat shell is not the concern. What can freeze is the water that stays behind after a bath, and the pipes that feed the tub. Walk-in tubs have more of both than a standard tub.

Should you drip faucets in Houston during a freeze?

This is the question that confuses people most, because the answer changes with the water system. In a statement reported by KPRC in 2025, Houston Public Works said it does not advise dripping faucets, because the city system relies on re-pump stations and widespread dripping could hurt water pressure for everyone. Homes served by a municipal utility district (MUD) or a water tower system are often told the opposite: keep a trickle running overnight. The City of Katy tells residents to let cold water drip from faucets served by pipes likely to freeze.

Your water bill shows which system serves you. If you are on City of Houston water, protect pipes with insulation and heat instead of dripping. If you are on a MUD or in Katy, follow your provider. Either way, the walk-in tub faucet does not need to drip if the bathroom is warm and the supply lines are protected.

A Houston freeze checklist for a walk-in tub bathroom

  1. Find the main shutoff now. In Harris County it is usually in a black box near the curb. Houston Public Works says to turn it clockwise to shut off water, and recommends doing that if you leave town during a freeze.
  2. Drain what holds water. After the last bath before a hard freeze, run the drain or purge cycle your manual calls for so the jet lines are not full.
  3. Insulate exposed pipes in the attic, garage and outside walls, and cover outdoor hose bibs.
  4. Keep the bathroom warm. Leave the bathroom door open to the heated house. Do not close off a bathroom on an outside wall to save heat on a 25°F night.
  5. Plan baths around the weather. Bathe before the cold night, not during a power outage, and keep a towel and robe within reach because the room will be cooler than usual.
  6. Check after the thaw. Look at the access panel, the ceiling below an upstairs bathroom and the floor around the tub for any sign of a leak.

Why does a freeze hit older adults harder?

A cold snap is not just a plumbing problem. Older adults lose body heat faster, and a bath in a cold room, or a wait in a tub while it drains, is a chilly few minutes. If a burst pipe or outage leaves the house without hot water, skip bathing for a day or two rather than risk a cold bath. If an older relative lives alone, check on them during a freeze, and make sure they know where the shutoff is or who to call. Freeze questions Houston families ask

Should I drip my faucets in Houston during a freeze?

It depends on who supplies your water. Houston Public Works has said it does not advise dripping faucets on the city system because widespread dripping can lower water pressure. Homes on a MUD or water tower system are often told to keep a trickle running, and the City of Katy advises letting cold water drip from faucets on freeze-prone pipes. Check your water bill to see which system you are on.

How many freezing nights does Houston get?

The National Weather Service Houston office lists an average of 11 nights at or below 32 degrees for the City of Houston, 8 at Hobby and 4 in Galveston. The average first freeze in Houston is around December 9 and the average last freeze around February 15. Houston averages 2 nights at or below 25 degrees, and reaches 25 degrees in 57% of years.

Can a walk-in tub freeze?

The tub shell will not crack from one cold night, but water left in jet lines, a pump or supply pipes on an outside wall can freeze if the bathroom gets cold enough. Run the drain or purge cycle your manual describes after the last bath before a hard freeze, keep the bathroom door open to the heated house, and protect the pipes that feed the tub.

Will a walk-in tub drain if the power goes out?

Most walk-in tubs drain by gravity, and the door opens once the water is out, but pumps, heated seats, inline heaters and some fast-drain systems stop without power. Check your model's manual, and skip baths during an outage if the heater or controls are not working.

Where is the water shutoff valve for a Harris County home?

The main shutoff is usually in a black box near the curb, and many homes also have a valve where the line enters the house. Houston Public Works says to turn the valve clockwise to shut off service, which it recommends if you leave town during a freeze. Find it before the cold arrives, not after a pipe breaks.

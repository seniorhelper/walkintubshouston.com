Spring families we help

Spring is a large, unincorporated area north of Houston, from the shops of Old Town Spring to neighborhoods along Louetta, Kuykendahl and the Grand Parkway. Many homes here were bought by young families in the 1980s and 1990s, and many of those owners now want to age in place. We also help families caring for a parent in a spare bedroom.

Our process is simple: a conversation, measuring and a detailed written quote. Nothing is ordered until you agree.

Spring houses and bathrooms

Klein and Champion Forest era homes

Many homes in the Klein and Champion Forest areas date from the 1970s through the 1990s. Bathrooms are often modest with standard alcoves, and some have original plumbing and tile. We plan for repairs behind the tub if we find them.

Newer homes near the Grand Parkway

Communities to the north and east have larger primary baths and many two-story plans. Bigger rooms can take a wider walk-in tub or a roll-in shower. Upstairs baths need a framing check and careful stair measurements.

Two counties, no city hall

Spring is unincorporated. Addresses south of Spring Creek are generally in Harris County; addresses north of the creek are in Montgomery County. There is no Spring city building department. County offices and your municipal utility district handle what permits and connection rules apply, and your HOA may set contractor hours. Have licensed trades handle plumbing and electrical changes, and confirm requirements for your address.

Spring Creek and Cypress Creek

Spring Creek, Cypress Creek and their tributaries have flooded neighborhoods in big storms, including Harvey. If your home took on water, tell us. We will inspect the wall and subfloor behind the tub and talk about moisture-resistant surround options. See our flood and storm guide for more.

Tub or shower in a Spring home?

If the person bathing can step a few inches and enjoys warm baths, a walk-in tub with a chair-height seat is a good fit. If they use a walker or need help, a low-threshold shower with a seat and handheld shower may be easier. Read walk-in tub or walk-in shower for the full comparison.

Small details that help on install day

Spring neighborhoods often have narrow driveways and street parking limits. Let us know if the truck should use a side entry, if there is a gate code, or if the home has a pet that should stay in a room. Plan for the bathroom to be out of service for the day.

For caregivers in Spring

If you are helping a parent bathe, an outward-opening door or a roll-in shower gives you room to stand beside them. Ask the aging agency for your county about caregiver support programs, and see our caregiver guide.

Spring by the numbers

The Census Bureau's Spring area (a census-designated place, not a city) is younger than the region: 9.4% of residents, about 6,300 people, were 65 or older in the 2019 to 2023 American Community Survey. The share of those older residents reporting a disability was 40.8%, above the metro rate of 32.8%, though small-area estimates like this carry a wide margin of error. For the Montgomery County side, the county figure was 13.5%. See the regional data page for comparisons.

Questions we hear about this

Is Spring in Harris County or Montgomery County?

Both. Generally, areas south of Spring Creek are in Harris County and areas north of it are in Montgomery County. Check your address, since it decides the aging agency and county offices.

Which aging agency should I call from Spring?

Harris County addresses use the Harris County Area Agency on Aging (832-393-4301). Montgomery County addresses use the H-GAC Area Agency on Aging (1-800-437-7396).

Do you install in Klein and Old Town Spring?

Yes, along with nearby Humble and The Woodlands. Call to confirm your address.

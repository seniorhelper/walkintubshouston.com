Who we help in Pearland

Pearland's older sections, around the original downtown and Old Town, have homes from the 1960s and 1970s, while Silverlake and Shadow Creek Ranch filled in later. That mix shapes our calls. Some customers need a walk-in tub to fit into a small original bath; others have a large primary bath with a garden tub they no longer feel safe stepping into.

We talk first, then measure and put the full scope in writing before anything is ordered.

Pearland bathrooms we see

Original-era homes

Older Pearland homes often have compact bathrooms with a standard alcove and a narrow door. We measure the bathroom door and hallway carefully, since a walk-in tub arrives in one piece. Compact models built for 60-inch spaces usually fit.

Master-planned neighborhoods

Silverlake, Shadow Creek Ranch and other newer areas often have roomy primary baths. These spaces can take a wider walk-in tub, an outward-door transfer tub or a full roll-in shower.

Clear Creek and flooding

Clear Creek runs along the north side of Pearland, and parts of the city have flooded in big storms, including Harvey. If your home took on water, we check the wall and subfloor behind the tub, and we may suggest a solid-surface surround instead of drywall and tile. Drainage in much of the area is managed by local drainage districts and the county. Our flood and storm guide explains how to plan.

Permits in Pearland

Inside Pearland city limits, the city's permitting and inspections office handles building, plumbing and electrical permits. A tub replacement that moves plumbing or adds a circuit is the kind of work that typically needs a licensed plumber or electrician and a permit. Addresses outside the city follow county and utility district rules. We confirm which applies to your address.

Local help for older adults

  • Brazoria and Fort Bend County addresses: H-GAC Area Agency on Aging, 1-800-437-7396.
  • Harris County addresses on Pearland's north edge: Harris County Area Agency on Aging, 832-393-4301.
  • Veterans: the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center is a short drive north; ask its Prosthetics office about HISA grants.
  • 2-1-1 Texas for local repair programs and other support.

Thinking ahead

Many Pearland customers are planning for the next ten years, not just the next month. If mobility may change, we talk about whether a walk-in tub will still work later, or whether a roll-in shower makes more sense now so you only remodel once. Our tub or shower guide helps with that decision.

Quick tips before your quote

Note which end of the tub has the faucet, the size of the water heater, and whether anyone in the house will still want to use a regular tub. In homes with only one full bath, the plan for install day matters too; many families arrange for a parent to bathe at a relative's home that day.

Help for families supporting a parent

If a parent is moving into your Pearland home, think about which bathroom they will use most and whether it is on the first floor. A first-floor hall bath converted to a walk-in tub or low-threshold shower is often the simplest path. Ask the H-GAC Area Agency on Aging about caregiver classes and respite programs too.

Pearland and Friendswood, by the numbers

In the Census Bureau's 2019 to 2023 American Community Survey, about 15,100 Pearland residents (12.0%) were 65 or older, and about 1,800 were 85 or older. Friendswood next door is older, with 15.8% of residents 65 or older and a median age of 39.7. Brazoria County as a whole had about 47,800 residents 65 and up. Which aging agency serves Pearland?

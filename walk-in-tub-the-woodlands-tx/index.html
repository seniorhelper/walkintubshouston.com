Safer bathing in The Woodlands

The Woodlands opened in the 1970s as a planned community built around its trees, and the early villages now have many longtime owners who plan to stay. We hear from retirees who want to keep enjoying a warm bath, from people recovering from surgery at nearby hospitals, and from families helping a parent who has moved closer.

Every project starts with a conversation and measuring. The written quote spells out the tub, removal, plumbing, electrical, wall finish and any permits, so there are no surprises.

Houses in The Woodlands: what matters for a tub

Early villages

Homes in Grogan's Mill, Panther Creek and Cochran's Crossing often date from the 1970s and 1980s. Bathrooms tend to be modest, with standard alcoves, and some have original plumbing. We check the drain and supply lines once the old tub is out and tell you plainly if anything needs attention.

Larger two-story homes

Many houses in Alden Bridge, Sterling Ridge and Creekside Park have primary suites on the second floor. We confirm floor framing for the weight of a filled walk-in tub, and measure the stairs. For someone who should avoid stairs, a first-floor bath conversion may be the better project.

Wooded lots

Tall pines are the signature of The Woodlands, and they also mean shade, pollen and leaf litter. Inside, that often means bathrooms that stay a little damper. A vented exhaust fan and a well-sealed surround matter here.

Rules in an unincorporated community

The Woodlands is not a city. It is governed by The Woodlands Township, and it sits mostly in Montgomery County with a part in Harris County. The Township's covenants and standards mostly concern the outside of homes, so interior bathroom work is usually not something they review, but check your deed restrictions if you are unsure. For plumbing and electrical changes, a licensed plumber and a licensed electrician should do the work and handle anything required by the county or your utility district.

Weather notes

Spring Creek and Panther Branch can rise in heavy rain, and hurricanes bring long power outages to the area just as they do to Houston. If your home has flooded before, we look closely at the wall and floor behind the old tub. See our storm planning page for how walk-in tubs behave without power.

Local help for older adults

  • Montgomery County addresses: H-GAC Area Agency on Aging, 1-800-437-7396, for benefits counseling, caregiver support and referrals.
  • Harris County addresses in Creekside Park: Harris County Area Agency on Aging, 832-393-4301.
  • Veterans: ask the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center and its community clinics about HISA grants.

A note on style

Many homeowners here care about how the bathroom looks. Walk-in tubs come in several finishes, and surrounds can be panels or tile. If you want the bathroom to feel like part of the house rather than a medical room, say so. We will show you options that are safe and still look good.

Have the size of your water heater, the bathroom floor (first or second), and which end of the tub holds the faucet. Photos of the tub wall and bathroom door help us suggest models quickly.

Older residents in The Woodlands

The Woodlands is older than the region overall. The Census Bureau's 2019 to 2023 American Community Survey counted about 16,900 residents 65 or older, 14.5% of the community, against 12.0% for the Houston metro, and a median age of 40.5. About 1,600 were 85 or older. With so many primary suites on the second floor in the larger homes described above, the stair check matters a great deal here. More numbers are on our Houston older adults data page.

Questions we hear about this

Do I need Township approval to install a walk-in tub in The Woodlands?

Township standards mainly cover the outside of homes, so interior bathroom work is usually not reviewed. Check your deed restrictions if unsure, and have licensed trades handle plumbing and electrical work.

Which Area Agency on Aging serves The Woodlands?

Most of The Woodlands is in Montgomery County, served by the H-GAC Area Agency on Aging (1-800-437-7396). Creekside Park addresses in Harris County use the Harris County Area Agency on Aging (832-393-4301).

Can you put a walk-in tub in an upstairs primary bath?

Often, yes, after we check framing and measure the stairs. For someone who should avoid stairs, converting a downstairs bath may be better.

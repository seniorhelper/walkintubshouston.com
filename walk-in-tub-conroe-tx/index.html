Conroe and the lake

Conroe is the county seat of Montgomery County and one of the fastest-growing cities in the region, but it also has deep roots. Some of our customers have lived near downtown for fifty years; others retired to Lake Conroe for the quiet and the water. Both groups want a bathroom that keeps them independent.

We begin with a conversation, measure in person and give a written quote listing every part of the job.

Conroe homes

Older homes near downtown

Houses near the historic downtown can be decades old, with small bathrooms and older plumbing. Some sit on pier-and-beam foundations rather than a slab, which changes how drains are reached. Compact walk-in tubs and tub-to-shower conversions usually fit.

Lake Conroe properties

Lake homes vary widely, from older weekend cabins turned full-time residences to large custom homes. Some have steps between rooms or narrow halls that matter when carrying in a one-piece tub. We measure the full path.

New subdivisions

Newer neighborhoods around Conroe have larger primary baths and many single-story plans, which are great for aging in place. These rooms can take a roomy walk-in tub or roll-in shower.

Water and weather

Lake Conroe is managed by the San Jacinto River Authority, and the West Fork of the San Jacinto River and local creeks can flood in big storms. Hurricanes also reach this far inland with heavy rain and long power outages. If your home has had water damage, tell us. Our storm planning guide explains how a walk-in tub behaves without power.

Permits in Conroe

Inside Conroe city limits, the city's building and permitting office handles building, plumbing and electrical permits. Moving drains, adding circuits or opening walls typically needs licensed trades and a permit. Many lake and rural addresses are outside city limits in unincorporated Montgomery County, where county and utility district rules apply. Some rural homes have private wells and septic systems; let us know, since that affects plumbing connections.

Local help for older adults

Montgomery County is served by the H-GAC Area Agency on Aging at 1-800-437-7396, which offers benefits counseling, caregiver support, care coordination and referrals for people 60 and older. Veterans can ask the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center or its community clinics about HISA home modification grants. 2-1-1 Texas lists local repair and support programs.

Choosing the right setup

If the person bathing enjoys a soak and can step a few inches, a walk-in tub is a fine fit. If a wheelchair is in use, a roll-in shower or a transfer tub with a wide outward door is often better. We will explain the trade-offs honestly.

Planning for help at home

If home health aides or family members will help with bathing, mention it when you call. An outward-opening door, a handheld shower on a slide bar and grab bars placed for both people make a big difference. The H-GAC Area Agency on Aging can point caregivers to support programs in Montgomery County.

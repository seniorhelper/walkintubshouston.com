Cypress, Texas

Walk-in tubs for Cypress and Cy-Fair homes

Cypress grew from ranch land to some of the busiest neighborhoods in northwest Harris County. We help families here make bathing safe again.

Who calls us from Cypress

We hear from Cypress households at every stage: couples in their sixties planning ahead, families who have moved a parent into a downstairs bedroom, and people recovering from a joint replacement. What they share is a bathroom that no longer fits how they move.

We start by phone, measure in person, and write a quote that includes every part of the work.

Cypress homes

Established neighborhoods

Areas such as Copperfield, Coles Crossing and Cypress Creek Lakes have homes from the 1980s through the 2000s, many with standard alcoves in hall baths and larger garden tubs in primary baths. Hall baths are often a good fit for compact walk-in tubs or tub-to-shower conversions.

Newer master-planned communities

Towne Lake, Bridgeland and similar communities have many two-story homes with large primary baths, which leaves room for wider tubs and roll-in showers. When the primary bath is upstairs, we check framing and the path for the tub.

An unincorporated area: what that means

Cypress is not a city. It is unincorporated Harris County, served by the county and by many municipal utility districts (MUDs). There is no city building department. The Harris County Office of the County Engineer is the place for county permit questions, and your MUD may have its own requirements for plumbing connections. Your HOA may have rules about contractor parking and hours. In practice, have a licensed plumber and electrician handle plumbing and electrical changes and confirm what is required for your address.

Cypress Creek and flooding

Cypress Creek and Little Cypress Creek flooded many homes during Harvey and other storms. If yours was one of them, tell us. We look at the wall cavity and subfloor behind the tub and talk about moisture-tolerant surrounds. Our flood and storm guide also explains how a walk-in tub works during a power outage.

Local help for older adults

Cypress is in Harris County, served by the Harris County Area Agency on Aging through the Houston Health Department at 832-393-4301. It offers benefits counseling, caregiver support, meals, in-home help and referrals for people 60 and older. Veterans can ask the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center or its community clinics about HISA home modification grants, and 2-1-1 Texas lists local repair programs.

Choosing for the long run

A family in a newer Cypress home may live there for twenty years. If that is your plan, think about the person bathing now and in ten years. A walk-in tub may be perfect today; if a wheelchair could be in the picture later, a roll-in shower might be the one remodel that lasts. We will be honest about both options.

Before the first call

Write down which end of the tub has the faucet, whether the bathroom is upstairs, the size of the water heater and anything the person bathing finds hard. If you have photos of the bathroom, they help us suggest models quickly.

Hot water in Cy-Fair homes

Many Cypress houses have a 40- or 50-gallon gas heater in the garage or attic. That may be enough for a compact walk-in tub but not for a deep or wide model. Send a photo of the heater label and we will tell you what to expect, or whether an in-line heater or a heater upgrade belongs in the quote.

Questions we hear about this

Do I need a permit for a walk-in tub in Cypress?

Cypress is unincorporated Harris County with no city building department. Permit questions go to the Harris County Office of the County Engineer, and your MUD may have requirements. Licensed trades should handle plumbing and electrical changes.

Which aging agency serves Cypress?

The Harris County Area Agency on Aging, run by the Houston Health Department, at 832-393-4301.

Do you serve Tomball and Jersey Village?

Yes. We serve Cypress, Tomball, Jersey Village, Fairbanks and much of Cy-Fair. Call to confirm your address.

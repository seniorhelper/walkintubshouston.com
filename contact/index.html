Contact questions

How fast will someone call me back?

A specialist reviews every quote request and calls you back as soon as possible. If you'd like to talk right away, call 1-888-779-2284 (BATH).

Do I need my measurements before I call?

No. Rough measurements help if you have them, but when you choose full installation our team measures the space, the path into your home and the utilities for you.

Is there any cost or obligation for a quote?

No. Talking with us and getting your quote is free, with no pressure and no obligation to buy.

Can I request a quote for a roll-in or walk-in shower?

Yes. Choose roll-in shower or walk-in shower on the form, or call us. Our handicap showers include a 30-year warranty on the entire shower, plus a 1-year labor warranty when we do the white-glove installation.

Do you only install, or can I buy supply-only?

Full installation is what we recommend for most customers, and we handle everything from demo to the final walkthrough. Supply-only orders are also available for experienced DIYers and contractors, with tech support included.
